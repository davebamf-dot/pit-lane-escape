Race control

Lights out and away we go.

Get your red race car (the glowing one, #1) out through the pit exit. The exit moves every lap. Drag any vehicle along its own lane; nothing turns, and tyre barriers can't be crossed. Keyboard: Tab to a vehicle, then use the arrow keys. The clock starts when the lights go out and pauses if you leave the tab. Par is the fewest moves possible; finishing on par is P1.
